## 🎒 The Student's Daily Pulse
> **Hajar's Note**: For students, the platform is their digital backpack. It must be organized, accessible, and arguably... *fun*. If it feels like a chore, we lose them.

### 🌅 07:30 AM - The "Pack Your Bag" Routine
1.  **Schedule Check**: "What do I have today?" (Math, Science, PE).
2.  **Homework Verification**: "Did I submit the Math sheet?" (Green Check = Relief).
3.  **Notifications**: "Did the teacher grade my essay yet?"

### 🏠 05:00 PM - The "Study Time" Routine
1.  **Pending Tasks**: Viewing the "To-Do" list for tomorrow.
2.  **Lesson Catch-up**: Re-watching a video from the Chemistry lesson they didn't fully understand.
3.  **Quiz Mode**: Taking a quick self-assessment to prep for an upcoming test.

## 🎯 Specific Case Workflows
> **Najlae's Note**: Gen Z/Alpha users expect "Gaming Quality" UI. Interactions should be snappy, colorful, and rewarding.

### 🏆 Case A: Completing Homework (The Quest)
- **Workflow**: Opening the task, solving it, uploading evidence.
- **UX Goal**: "Mission Feedback." Progress bars that fill up. Confetti animation on submission.
- **Action**: 'Mark as Done' feels like leveling up.

### 📉 Case B: Reviewing Grades (The Feedback Loop)
- **Workflow**: Checking results and teacher comments.
- **UX Goal**: Don't just show a number. Show "Growth." Use trends charts (line graphs) instead of static tables.
- **Action**: "Ask Teacher" button available directly next to the grade for clarification.

### 🧪 Case C: Interactive Learning (The Lab)
- **Workflow**: Engaging with digital biology tools or math simulations.
- **UX Goal**: Full-screen immersive mode. Dark background to focus on the content.

## 🛠️ Two Workflow Strategy Options
*Proposed by Hajar (PM) & Visualized by Najlae (UX/UI)*

| Option | Personality | PM Focus (Hajar) | UI Vision (Najlae) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. The Explorer** | **Self-Paced** | "My Journey." Encourages curiosity and browsing past lessons. | Map-style navigation (like a game map), unlocking "Nodes" (Lessons) as they progress. |
| **2. The Achiever** | **Goal-Oriented** | "My Performance." Focus on deadlines, grades, and keeping a streak. | Dashboard with daily streaks, badge collections, and leaderboard-style aesthetics (if socially appropriate). |

## ✨ UX/UI Premium Guidelines (Najlae's Signature)
To make the student want to log in, we implement:
- **Gamification Elements**: Subtle badges for "Early Bird Submission" or "Perfect Attendance."
- **Focus Timer**: A built-in Pomodoro timer for study sessions.
- **Mood Check-in**: A simple emoji slider on login "How are you feeling today?" that gives data to the counselor (but looks cute).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Hajar's Business Verdict**: **Option 2 (The Achiever)** drives engagement metrics and homework completion rates, which are key deliverables for the school's reputation.
