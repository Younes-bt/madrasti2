## 👩‍🏫 The Teacher's Daily Pulse
> **Hajar's Note**: Teachers are the engine of the school. Their dashboard must reduce administrative friction so they can focus on *teaching*. We prioritize "Classroom Readiness."

### 🌅 07:45 AM - The "Morning Coffee" Check
1.  **Schedule Sync**: A quick glance at today's timetable.
    - *Action*: Click to view lesson plan for the 1st period.
2.  **Attendance Alert**: Check if any students have pre-notified absences.
3.  **Materials Prep**: Verify that homework for today's classes is ready to be collected/reviewed.

### 🍎 02:00 PM - The "Deep Work" Session
1.  **Grading Batch**: Reviewing submitted exercises and assigning marks.
2.  **Parent Messaging**: Replying to two urgent queries from concerned parents.
3.  **Content Creation**: Drafting a new interactive lesson for next week.

---

## 🎯 Specific Case Workflows
> **Najlae's Note**: Teachers switch contexts rapidly (Grading -> Teaching -> disciplining). The UI must visually signal these mode switches.

### 📚 Case A: Lesson Planning (The Creator Mode)
- **Workflow**: Uploading resources, defining objectives, attaching exercises.
- **UX Goal**: A focused "Studio Interface" with drag-and-drop capabilities.
- **Action**: "Publish to Class" with a satisfying confirmation animation.

### 📝 Case B: Grading & Feedback (The Focus Mode)
- **Workflow**: Rapidly going through student submissions.
- **UX Goal**: Split-screen view (Student Work vs. Grading Rubric). Keyboard shortcuts for speed.
- **Action**: "Voice Note Feedback" for a personal touch.

### 📢 Case C: Class Announcement (The Broadcaster)
- **Workflow**: Informing students about a test or change in schedule.
- **UX Goal**: "Composer Card" that floats above content, resembling social media posting.

## 🛠️ Two Workflow Strategy Options
*Proposed by Hajar (PM) & Visualized by Najlae (UX/UI)*

| Option | Personality | PM Focus (Hajar) | UI Vision (Najlae) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. The Academic** | **Efficiency First** | "Grades and Stats." Focus on deliverables and performance metrics. | Spreadsheet-style grids, high data density, minimal colors (focus on Red/Green indicators). |
| **2. The Mentor** | **Relationship First** | "Students are people." Focus on individual progress and communication. | Card-based layout with student avatars, warmer colors, "Engagement Pulse" visualizations. |

---

## ✨ UX/UI Premium Guidelines (Najlae's Signature)
To empower the teacher, we implement:
- **The "Classroom Deck"**: A cover-flow style view of classes, making it fun to swipe between groups.
- **Smart Grading Tags**: One-click tags like "Great Job", "Needs Review", "Check Spellings" that auto-fill comments.
- **Zen Mode**: A toggle that hides all sidebar menus when entering grades to prevent distraction.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Hajar's Business Verdict**: **Option 2 (The Mentor)** is the future of education. It aligns with premium private schooling where personal attention is the key selling point.
