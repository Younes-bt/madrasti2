## 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 The Parent's Daily Pulse
> **Hajar's Note**: Parents are the *clients*. They pay the fees and judge the value. Their experience must be "Reassurance & Pride." They need to feel connected without being overwhelmed.

### 🔔 10:00 AM - The "Safety Check"
1.  **Attendance Notification**: "Your child arrived safely at school." (The most important daily touchpoint).
2.  **Activity Feed**: "Younes answered a question correctly in Math today." (Positive reinforcement).

### 🌙 08:00 PM - The "Dinner Table" Prep
1.  **Homework Monitor**: Checking what assignments are due tomorrow to help their child.
2.  **Behavior Log**: Quickly scanning for any notes from teachers (good or bad).
3.  **Communication**: Reading a circular about next week's field trip.

---

## 🎯 Specific Case Workflows
> **Najlae's Note**: The Parent App is a "concierge" service. It should feel as premium as a banking or lifestyle app.

### 💳 Case A: Fee Payment (The Transaction)
- **Workflow**: Viewing invoices and making a secure payment.
- **UX Goal**: Trust and Clarity. Clean typography, secure lock icons, clear distinction between "Paid" and "Due."
- **Action**: One-tap "Download Receipt" for their records.

### 📊 Case B: Report Card Day (The Milestone)
- **Workflow**: Detailed review of term performance.
- **UX Goal**: A digital "Unwrapping" experience. Don't just dump a PDF. Show a summarized view first with key highlights.
- **Action**: "Schedule Meeting" button prominent if grades are below a threshold.

### 🚌 Case C: Transport Tracking (The Peace of Mind)
- **Workflow**: Checking where the bus is during drop-off/pick-up.
- **UX Goal**: Real-time map view with a friendly vehicle icon.
- **Action**: "Near Home" alert configuration.

## 🛠️ Two Workflow Strategy Options
*Proposed by Hajar (PM) & Visualized by Najlae (UX/UI)*

| Option | Personality | PM Focus (Hajar) | UI Vision (Najlae) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. The Reviewer** | **Monitoring** | "Show me the data." Grades, attendance logs, financial history. | Clean lists, detailed history views, filterable reports. |
| **2. The Partner** | **Collaborative** | "Involve me." Tips on how to help at home, direct chat with teachers. | Feed-style interface (like a social timeline), "Moments" sharing from school to home. |

---

## ✨ UX/UI Premium Guidelines (Najlae's Signature)
To justify the premium nature of the school, we implement:
- **Kid Switcher**: A seamless, beautiful transition animation when switching between siblings profiles.
- **"Fridge" Gallery**: A section to save/favorite their child's best work or art uploaded by the teacher.
- **Smart Summaries**: AI-generated weekly summaries "Younes had a great week in Science, but needs help with Algebra."

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Hajar's Business Verdict**: **Option 2 (The Partner)** creates loyalty. When parents feel they are partners in the education process, retention rates skyrocket.
